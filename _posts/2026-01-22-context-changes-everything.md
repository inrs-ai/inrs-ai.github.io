title: "Context changes everything"
date: 2026-01-22
categories: blog
---

1. To me, it is essential to avoid looking at things in isolation. The same event, statement, or data can have completely different interpretations depending on the context.
2. When trying to understand others, it is important to pay attention to the context (such as cultures, situations, and the relationship between the parties), otherwise misunderstandings can easily arise.
3. The 'absolute truth' detached from specific contexts is often unreliable.
